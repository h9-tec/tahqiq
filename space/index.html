Tahqiq

Tahqiq (تحقيق): the slowest, most exact recitation, every letter given its full right; also the Arabic word for verification. Open models on the same public test sets as Elm's leaderboard, re-run with one harness, two published normalizers, numeral and entity probes, and a 95% confidence interval on every number. Submit any open model below and it gets queued on our GPUs.

Method

Every system is run zero-shot, as released, with the decode settings recorded in its run.json. Hypotheses and references go through the same published normalizer. WER and CER are corpus-level. Every number carries a 95% utterance-level percentile bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples). Test sets are the six public ones used by Elm's Open Universal Arabic ASR Leaderboard, reconstructed utterance for utterance from Elm's manifests (MGB-2 pending); audio stays on our machine, only outputs are published. The common slice is a fixed, seeded, dialect-stratified sample of about 1,000 utterances per set.

Normalizers. elm is a line-for-line port of Elm's, tested for string equality against the original, so that column is comparable to Elm's board. tolerant adds NFC, tatweel, alef/alef-maksura/ta-marbuta/hamza folding and a published spelling-variant table.

Probes. Numeral: references with digits or an unambiguous spoken number word, scored on digit-string equality with number words mapped on both sides. Entity: reference PERS/LOC/ORG spans from CAMeL-Lab's NER tagger (score ≥ 0.8, published side file), scored by containment after tolerant normalization.

Scope. Open-weight models and global vendors only. Vendors domiciled in the GCC appear only after written opt-in. This page reports measured values and makes no other claims. Full details, code, manifests and every per-utterance output: github.com/h9-tec/tahqiq and tahqiq-results.
